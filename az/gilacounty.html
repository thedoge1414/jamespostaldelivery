Service Discontinuation Notice: Gila County

Please be advised that James Postal Delivery no longer offers general postal and courier services within Gila County.

Important Notice: Standard pickup and delivery operations in Gila County have been suspended effective immediately.

Do you have an OOSR Number?

Unless you have been issued an official OOSR (Out-of-Service-Region) Number, you are no longer able to schedule, send, or receive deliveries through our service in Gila County.

If you believe you received this message in error or need assistance obtaining an OOSR exemption number, please contact customer support.
