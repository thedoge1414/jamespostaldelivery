JPD is no longer available in Gila County.

Starting 8/31/26, all of Gila County will no longer be able to use JPD services. If this effects any packages you are supposed to receive, you will still receive them. However, if you are sending packages, you will be requested to use other services, and our system will cancel your package. If you have already sent any packages, they will be delivered. Any packages that are undeliverable will be returned at the cost of the shipper. If you wish to have your location receive packages after 8/31/26, you will be required to buy a $10/mo service subscription to have your packages sent via JPD to you forwarded to local carriers for final delivery.
