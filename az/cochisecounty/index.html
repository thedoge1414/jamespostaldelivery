How Incoming & Outgoing Mail Moves

Mail originating outside or entering Cochise County moves through a hub-and-spoke routing sequence:

  1. Entry & Hub Intake: Inbound standard ground items reach the regional Processing & Distribution Center (P&DC) in Tucson.
  2. County Line Feeder: Early morning dispatch trucks transport sorted mail down the I-10 and Highway 90 corridors into local distribution hubs in Sierra Vista and Huachuca City.
  3. Local Delivery / Re-Routing: Carriers perform local route drops. Outbound items collected standardly exit back to the regional hub the same evening, creating a total turnaround of 1 to 2 business days for standard ground handling.
