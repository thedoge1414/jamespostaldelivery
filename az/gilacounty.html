JPD services in Gila County have officially concluded.

What You Need to Know

Effective August 31st at 12:00 AM MST, JPD services will cease operations across all regions of Gila County. This transition is part of an upcoming regional restructuring effort.

We recommend all active service users and local stakeholders take necessary steps prior to the cutoff date to ensure uninterrupted coverage through alternative channels.

Transition & Alternative Options

If you currently rely on JPD coverage in Gila County, please refer to the primary alternative providers and resources below:

Frequently Asked Questions

Will my data remain accessible?

Account data will remain available for export until August 31st. After this time, local regional profiles will be archived according to standard data privacy policies.

Who do I contact for active case files?

Please submit a records request using the contact form below prior to the deadline to request transfer of ongoing materials.
